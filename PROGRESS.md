## Pre-registration: M2 hypothesis H1 (written 2026-10-13, BEFORE recording any layout-v2 session)
- **H1:** with pose-free features, `gaze_blend` (iris xy + the 14 eye blendshapes = 18 features) with ridge alpha=100 has lower cross-session error than `gaze` (iris xy, 4 features) with ridge alpha=10.
- **Data:** three new sessions recorded with calibration layout `v2` (25 targets), at different times of day/lighting, each tagged with `--note`. **Inclusion rule, fixed now:** `meta.layout == "v2"` and window `[0,0,1470,956]`. No session is dropped after seeing results; a session is dropped only for an objective failure (< 80% valid frames, or the target-safety abort), logged with the reason.
- **Primary (confirmatory) analysis:** leave-one-session-out. For each v2 session, train on the pooled targets (calibration and held-out) of every *other* clean session (the older clean sessions plus the other v2 sessions) and test on **all 25 targets of the held-out session**. Unit of analysis = target. Per-target error = mean over frames of Euclidean error in degrees of visual angle. Both configs are evaluated on identical targets (paired).
- **Primary statistic:** mean of the per-target paired differences d = err(`gaze` a=10) - err(`gaze_blend` a=100) (positive favors H1), with a 95% bootstrap CI resampling targets (10,000 resamples, fixed seed).
- **Success criterion (all three required):** (a) the CI lower bound is > 0; (b) the mean improvement is >= 0.25 deg; (c) `gaze_blend` is better in at least 2 of the 3 held-out sessions. Otherwise H1 is reported as **not supported**, with no re-tuning on these sessions.
- **Caveats, stated up front:**
  - The three clean M1 sessions (including Independent analysis #2) suggested the hypothesis, and alpha=100 was also suggested by them; they are exploratory and never confirmatory.
  - The comparison changes features and alpha together, so a win does not say which caused it. Secondary diagnostic configs (reported, not part of the criterion): `gaze` alpha=100 and `gaze_blend` alpha=10.
  - Three sessions carry little session-level information. A secondary sensitivity analysis uses a two-level bootstrap (resample sessions, then targets).
  - Everything else computed is exploratory and labeled so (`confirmatory=0` in `results.csv`).

## Guided calibration (added 2026-10-13, at Abhimanyu's request, before v2 sessions 2 and 3)

Extra qualifying sessions are ignored; selection is never by results.

**H2: calibrating in the lighting you will use beats a pool from other lighting.**
- For each test session T in {F2, B2}: train on all 25 targets of the **same-lighting twin** (F2 <- F, B2 <- B) versus the **other-lighting session** (F2 <- B, B2 <- F); test on all 25 targets of T. Equal training size (one session each), identical targets (paired).
- Statistic: mean over the 50 test targets of d = err(other-lighting) - err(same-lighting), degrees; 95% bootstrap CI over targets (10,000 resamples, seed 0). **Success (all three):** (a) CI lower bound > 0; (b) mean d >= 0.5 deg; (c) d > 0 in both test sessions. Secondary: two-level (session then target) CI.

**H3: sessions that pass the brightness gate (face >= 60/255) are more accurate than sessions below it.**
- Within-session: each session trains on its own 16 calibration targets and tests on its own 9 interior held-out targets. Groups: **gated = {F2, B2}**, **below gate = {D1, D2}** (18 targets each).
- Statistic: mean error(below) - mean error(gated), degrees, 95% bootstrap CI resampling targets within each group (10,000 resamples, seed 0). **Success (all three):** (a) CI lower bound > 0; (b) difference >= 1.5 deg; (c) every gated session has a lower mean error than every dim session (4 of 4 pairs).

**Clarification (implementation, 2026-10-13, before any round-2 recording):** the "front-lit" label itself requires face >= 100 and face/background >= 0.9, so the written F2 range "label front-lit and face 93-155" is effectively face **100-155**; the code applies both written conditions and the 93 lower bound is unreachable. No change in intent.

**Where the gate comes from:** the only three conditions measured so far (exploratory, within-session error): face brightness 37 -> 7.3-8.6 deg; 78 -> 3.0-3.5 deg; 124 -> 1.3-1.7 deg. 60 sits between the dim and backlit sessions (the geometric mean of 37 and 78 is about 54, rounded up); >= 100 is the recommended (non-blocking) level. It is a three-point inference and the 37-78 range is untested.

**Caveats (up front):** the threshold, the 0.5 and 1.5 deg margins and both hypotheses come from three exploratory sessions of one person in one room, so this tests "this user, this room", not a population. Two sessions per group carry little session-level information (hence the 4-of-4 and both-sessions guards and the two-level CI). F, B and the gated sessions also differ in the face/background ratio, a confound H3 cannot separate from brightness. Passing H3 shows below-60 sessions are worse, not that 60 is the exact optimum. Failing H2 or H3 is reported as not supported, with no re-tuning on these sessions. All other output is exploratory and labelled so.

## Step 2b implementation notes (2026-10-13)
- Face crops: native-resolution JPEG q90, face-mesh bbox x1.5 squared, unflipped; sidecar `<session>.faces.npz` (`offsets`, `data`, `box`, `quality`), row i = session row i, empty entry when no face; mode 0600; `eval/faces.py` (`crop_box`, `encode_crop`, `save_faces`, `FaceCrops`, `estimate_mb`). The sidecar is not a session (`session_files()` skips `*.faces.npz`). `calibrate.py` prints an estimate before and the actual size after. Measured live: 27 KB per crop at a 437 px crop (about 21 MB per session at that distance). The 2026-10-13 dry run (331 px crops, 761 frames) came out at **15.3 MB** (20 KB per crop), above the first estimate, so the estimate was recalibrated: about 10-15 MB per v2 session at the usual distance.
- **Replay caveat (measured live, numbers only, 60 frames):** the landmarker re-finds the face in 60/60 stored crops, but the iris features it produces on a crop differ from the live full-frame result by about 14-24% of their spread across targets even for a **lossless** crop; JPEG q90 adds only a few points and q70 is no worse than q90. So the gap comes from re-running the landmarker on a cropped image, not from compression. That gap is similar in size to the effects measured here (a feature shift of 0.003-0.005 is roughly 1 deg of gaze). Use rule: **compare image-level variants against the replay baseline (same crop pipeline, paired), never against the live numbers**; absolute accuracy from replayed images is not comparable to live accuracy.
- Gate: `LIGHT_MIN_FACE` 60 (blocks SPACE below it), `LIGHT_GOOD_FACE` 100 (tip), `BACKLIT_RATIO` 0.6 (backlight tip, never a gate); `CheckItem` has `tip` and `value`; the checklist draws a live brightness bar with the 60 and 100 marks and rows grow with their hint and tips. `lighting_warning` now uses the same 60.
- Round-2 code: `eval/prereg2.py` (`lighting_class`, `select_sessions`, `run_h2`, `run_h3`), `stats.bootstrap_diff_ci`; sessions that started before 2026-10-13T04:15:26 (the pre-registration commit) can never be test sessions; tests plant effects (a planted H2 and H3 effect is detected; a null is not; the 4-of-4 guard works).

## Display facts (locked in `ScreenConfig`)
- Panel (Apple tech specs, support.apple.com/en-us/126320): 13.6" Liquid Retina, 2560x1664 at 224 ppi (= 29.0 x 18.9 cm; Quartz/EDID gives 29.06 x 18.90). Apple: only the TOP corners are rounded (bottom square) and the actual viewable area is smaller than the 13.6" rectangle.
- Default mode is "looks like 1470x956" (rendered at 2x = 2940x1912, downscaled to the panel), so 1 pt = 0.01975 cm. Pixels everywhere in this project are logical points.
- Measured via AppKit on this Mac (MacBook Air Mac17,3, M5, 16 GB): `safeAreaInsets.top` = 32 pt (menu bar/notch strip); notch spans x = 646..825 pt (179 pt wide, 32 pt tall). `ScreenConfig.from_system()` reads these and the physical size (Quartz). Viewing distance is an assumed 60 cm.
- Corner radius: NOT published by Apple and no public API; `corner_radius_pt = 24` is a generous ASSUMPTION used only to keep targets away. `target_is_safe()` rejects targets within the ring radius of the edges, the 32 pt strip or the rounded corners; `calibrate.py` aborts if any are unsafe. `--check-screen` draws the four extreme-safe rings plus the notch outline.

## Gotchas and decisions
- Pose sign conventions on the UNFLIPPED frame (what recordings use): pitch negative = head tilted back/looking up; yaw and roll signs are inverted relative to the old mirrored viewer. The viewer now processes the raw frame and mirrors for display only (landmarks drawn at 1 - x). The webcam sits low, so neutral pitch reads about -2 to -13. Head pose assumes R = Ry(yaw) Rx(pitch) Rz(roll) with scale removed.
- No component mirrors the frame it processes; sessions record `mirrored: false`.
- Landmarks: 478 points; right iris 468-472 (center 468), left iris 473-477 (center 473); x/y normalized. VIDEO mode needs strictly increasing timestamps (`FaceTracker.process` enforces this).
- Blendshapes (52) are recorded per frame; names are in `meta["blendshape_names"]`. Candidate subset = `BLEND_FEATURE_NAMES` (eyeLook In/Out/Up/Down, Blink, Squint, Wide, L/R = 14). Tracker cost measured live: median 10.1 ms with blendshapes vs 7.2 ms without (p95 12.6 vs 13.4), fine for 30 fps.
- Quartz/Cocoa pyobjc were pulled in at M1 (CLAUDE.md originally said M3) for screen geometry and the overlay window.
- `eval/targets.py` held-out function is `held_out_points` (a `test_` name would be collected by pytest).
- No model save/load yet: the overlay refits from a session on start.
- Deps: mediapipe 1.1.0, opencv-python 5.0.0.93 (mediapipe also pulls opencv-contrib-python; harmless), numpy 2.5.4.
- Settle frames (0.7 s per target) are not recorded; M2 step 6 (lag) would need re-recording.
